# Risk Disclosure

QuietClaw is experimental software.

It is designed to keep processing local by default, but if you enable a third-party provider, selected content may be sent to that provider.

Using QuietClaw can involve important risks, including:

- your prompts, files, context, or outputs may contain personal, confidential, or regulated information;
- if you enable a third-party provider, that information may be transferred to and processed by the provider you selected;
- models can generate incorrect, misleading, biased, offensive, or unsafe output;
- content from documents, websites, or messages may contain malicious or adversarial instructions that influence model behavior (sometimes called prompt injection);
- local files may persist longer than intended because of caches, logs, crash files, backups, hibernation, or software error;
- device compromise, local malware, shared-device access, debugging tools, or other system behavior may expose data;
- some risks are not yet known.

Use QuietClaw only if you understand and accept these limitations.
