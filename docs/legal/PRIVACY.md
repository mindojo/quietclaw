# Privacy Notice

QuietClaw is designed as local-first software. This notice explains what the Software is designed to process locally, what may be sent to third-party providers if you enable them, what optional diagnostics may be generated, and what controls are available to you.

## Scope

This notice applies to QuietClaw, including its application binaries, source distribution, local services, and related documentation.

## Local processing by default

QuietClaw is designed to process prompts, attachments, retrieved context, intermediate outputs, settings, provider configuration, and local working state on your device by default.

## Third-party provider transfers

If you enable a third-party provider, QuietClaw may send selected prompts, attachments, retrieved context, output-control parameters, and related request metadata directly to that provider in order to obtain model responses. The exact content sent depends on your settings, selected workflow, enabled tools, and the action you take.

## Diagnostics, telemetry, and support

QuietClaw may generate local logs, crash artifacts, and related diagnostic information. Optional telemetry or crash uploads, if offered, are separate choices and are disabled by default unless you affirmatively enable them.

Support bundles, if generated, should be reviewed by you before they are sent.

## Retention target

QuietClaw is designed to expire certain local working data after approximately 24 hours. This is a target retention model and not a guarantee. Data may persist longer because of logs, caches, crash files, backups, sync state, hibernation, operating-system behavior, or software error.

## Your controls

Depending on the build and features enabled, you may be able to:

- use local-only mode;
- enable or disable third-party providers;
- clear local history and caches;
- disable optional telemetry and crash uploads;
- review support bundles before sending them.
